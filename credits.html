01 / PRODUCTION CREDITS

From drawings to the screen.

Character artwork

Original character drawings by Amlesh Sahoo. Character renders, angle studies, and supporting storyboard images created with Google Gemini ↗.

Animated shots

AI-generated animation created with Kling AI ↗, using character references and scene prompts selected and directed by Amlesh.

Narration

Synthesized narration generated with ElevenLabs ↗. The voice is AI-generated and is not a recording of Amlesh speaking.

Video editing

Edited and creatively directed by Amlesh Sahoo, with AI-assisted production tools. Runway ↗ was used for a lighting refinement to the mother’s blessing scene.

Website design

Website concept and layout by Amlesh Sahoo. Visual styling, beautification, and technical implementation by OpenAI Codex ↗, under Amlesh’s direction.

Production assistance

ChatGPT / Codex assisted with planning, technical video assembly, pacing adjustments, caption synchronization, and website development. Amlesh guided the creative choices and revisions.

03 / SOURCE & INSPIRATION

Rooted in a personal experience.

Primary source

Amlesh Sahoo’s personal badminton narrative, repurposed for an Iowa State University multimodal assignment. Personal and family reference photographs were supplied by Amlesh.

Creative influences

Jujutsu Kaisen, Haikyu!!, and Kuroko’s Basketball informed the desired atmosphere, sports storytelling, and dramatic pacing. These titles are acknowledged as creative influences, not as creators or sponsors of this project.

Process note

This is a human-directed, AI-assisted student production. The credits above distinguish original contributions, generated media, and technical assistance. Linked tool names lead to their official websites.
